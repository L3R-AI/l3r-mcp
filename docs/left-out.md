# What L3R leaves out

To protect people's privacy and safety, the L3R MCP server does not show the facts below. This list is also at [mcp.l3r.io/left-out](https://mcp.l3r.io/left-out).

- **Owners, values and sales:** who owns a home and what it is worth or sold for stay private.
- **Holdings by owner:** properties are never grouped or keyed by owner.
- **Private people's names:** only public officials and professionals in their public role, such as architects; buildings named after people keep their names.
- **Shelter locations:** women's, domestic violence, youth and homeless shelters are never shown or confirmed at any address.
- **Housing labels that reveal health:** HIV/AIDS housing shows only as "Supportive housing", without its name. Senior, veterans and assisted living housing show with their names.
- **Code enforcement:** counts, how many are open and the newest date only; never case details, who complained or notes.
- **Permit descriptions:** only words from a reviewed list, so personal details are not repeated.
- **Prices of development rights:** transfers are shown, not what was paid.

If you see something that should not be shown, email [mcp@l3r.io](mailto:mcp@l3r.io).
